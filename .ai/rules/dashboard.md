# Dashboard

## Keep dashboards card-based and comparisons explicit
Use the attached card-based hierarchy: individual metric cards, a visible What changed section, traffic, pages, and compact breakdowns. Do not replace it with Focus or Explore mega-panels. Trend labels must show an actual percentage and previous value, or a neutral explanation that prior data is unavailable.
