# Pages

## Keep the dashboard analytics-first
The dashboard should lead with traffic, key metrics, and compact audience breakdowns. Installation guidance belongs in onboarding/settings; do not add the “Add Peekchimp to your site” promo card back to the dashboard.

## Explain organic search as a three-question story
Keep the default Search Console dashboard view focused on discovery, post-click engagement, and goal value in plain language. Show one next action, and place landing-page/query tables behind collapsed progressive disclosure.
